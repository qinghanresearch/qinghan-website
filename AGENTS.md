# Website editorial rules

Scope: this Quarto personal website. These rules reflect the user's explicit editorial direction.

## Editorial priority

Prioritize clarity, documentation, and intellectual continuity over awards, publicity, photo quantity, or self-promotion. Do not make every project visually identical, and do not impose equal numbers or types of images or a conventional gallery on every page.

Before adding any photo, media report, competition image, screenshot, recognition, or illustration, ask: “Does this help the visitor understand what Qing actually did?” If not, leave it out. Generic conceptual artwork must not stand in for actual project evidence.

## Evidence appropriate to each project

- Hongshan: the published book, translation materials, museum and archaeology/heritage work.
- TuringPal: product prototypes, devices, documented deployment or system diagrams tied to actual work.
- Restaurant systems: interfaces, workflows, and documented real deployment.
- Academic research: thesis, research diagrams, or selected research materials appropriate for public display.

Do not invent missing evidence or infer deployment, adoption, endorsement, or collaboration from an image. Captions should explain what an item documents. A book photographed in a museum does not establish museum acquisition, endorsement, or display.

## Research and practice

Keep research questions, methods, and interpretations distinct from project outputs and evidence. Explain both what Qing contributed and why the work is intellectually relevant, without turning practical experience into an automatically authorized research dataset.

For the Hongshan translation, context may address heritage interpretation, translation across languages and cultures, public archaeology, representations of archaeological knowledge, and international communication of Chinese archaeological heritage. Use “The book in context,” not “Around the book.”

## Content and publication boundaries

Preserve confirmed roles and source attribution. Distinguish published translations from publications in preparation and from scholarly authorship. Never invent publication dates, institutional affiliations, awards, or outcomes.

Do not copy private source materials, applications, raw fieldnotes, BP, credentials, or complete evidence dossiers into the website. Work locally unless publication is explicitly requested.

## Research presentation and routine updates

Lead with the research object and question, then methods, materials, and current status. Historical inquiry and anthropological methods should be visible in the substance of the writing. Keep language concrete and avoid promotional slogans or unsupported novelty claims. Preserve Qing's own identity and confirmed qualifications.

When a detail page changes, check its summaries on Home, Research, Writing, About, and CV. Preserve established URLs when a project title evolves. Translation as Governance uses `research/hongshan.qmd`. Do not turn tentative corpus observations or research blueprints into established results.
